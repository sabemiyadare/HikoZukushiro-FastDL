zm_hakureijinja - Zombie Mod
original map by GreenTea for Sven-Coop (Touhou_Hakureijinja)
CS 1.6 Zombie Mod conversion by H1k0Pwn5j00 (Hiko Zukushiro)

Various humans from Gensokyo had fled to the Hakurei Shrine in order to survive the zombie apocalypse,
but little do they know that Hakurei Reimu and Marisa Kirisame aren't there to help them, so they
are forced to survive on their own.

Humans: Survive the zombie apocalypse until the timer runs out, or kill all the zombies. (You should already know this by know)

Zombies: Infect all of the humans to zombify the entirety of Hakurei Shrine, duh!

Map dedicated to all Anime, Touhou, Counter-Strike, and Zombie Plague fans.
